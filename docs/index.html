      05 — Skills

      What the market is asked to supply

      Requirements are read from each advert's Qualifications block and nowhere else — the one part of a posting that states demand rather than describing the team or the job.

      Capability asked for

      An advert is counted once per capability, so these do not sum to the number of adverts.

      Open-list clauses — not a capability

      These two clauses enumerate acceptable options rather than state a demand, so they are kept out of every ranking, index and crosstab above. Counted as skills they would be the two largest bars on the page while saying nothing about what the job needs.

      Most-named requirements

      The entry bar

      Median years by band

      Where an advert states several thresholds, the lowest is taken — that is the bar it will actually accept. The manager band asks for years of people management, which is a different clock from the technical bands.

        Hiring Signal

        Method

        Every page of the public search index on careers.microsoft.com is walked, then each posting's detail record is read for profession, discipline, role type, employment type, work-site policy, required travel and full description. Clusters are matched by keyword across all of those fields.
